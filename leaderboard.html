🏆 Awards Backfill

Recalculates All-Time MVP / Top Fragger / Top Shitter / Assist King / OBJ MVP award counts, plus fixes Hill/S, Ball/S, and Caps/S to divide by series that actually had that gametype (not total series played), for the 4v4 leaderboard — from every series stored in History. Safe to re-run — it always recalculates from scratch rather than adding on top, and takes an automatic snapshot first.

🗑️ Remove a Series

Unlike restoring a backup — which undoes every change since that point — this surgically undoes just one series' contribution to the 4v4 leaderboard (stats, record, and any awards it earned), no matter how many series have been added since. The series stays visible in History, just marked as removed. Takes an automatic snapshot first.

👻 Find Orphaned Names

Scans every series in History for the 4v4 leaderboard and lists any player name that doesn't match a current leaderboard entry — usually an old gamertag left behind after a name change that was later merged, whose History entries are now invisible to any lookup under the current name.

If a player's Hill/S, Ball/S, or Caps/S looks impossibly low or high, this walks their entire History and shows exactly which series counted toward those rates and why any were missed — usually an OCR name-spelling inconsistency in one specific report.
